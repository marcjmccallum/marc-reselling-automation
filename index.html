Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Marc Reselling Automation

Marc Reselling Automation is a private application used to assist with managing the owner's eBay selling activity and inventory.

Information Accessed

The application may access information from the eBay account authorised by the application owner, including listing and inventory information required to perform its functions.

Use of Information

Information obtained through eBay is used solely to provide inventory and listing-management functionality for the authorised account.

Data Storage

The application does not persistently store eBay user data. Information obtained from eBay is processed only as required to perform the requested application functions.

Sharing of Information

Information obtained through eBay is not sold, rented or provided to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and information processed by the application.

Changes to this Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of Marc Reselling Automation change.
